A new preprint with Ville Sarpo is available: "Two Channels of a Dual Mandate" examines how a Finnish university of applied sciences team carries out its teaching and RDI mandates in everyday interaction. Read it on [Research Square](https://doi.org/10.21203/rs.3.rs-11150277/v1) or on the [publications](/publications/) page. The preprint has not yet undergone peer review.
