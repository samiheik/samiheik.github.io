A new preprint with Henrik Bellhäuser, Esther van der Stappen and Mohammed Saqr is available on [SSRN](https://ssrn.com/abstract=7679998): "Individual differences in submission timeliness in technology-enhanced learning: A growth mixture modeling approach". Using data from 262 students, we identify four distinct trajectories of keeping self-set deadlines and show that students who planned alike differ in how they carry out their plans. The preprint has not yet undergone peer review.
